Common questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes — upgrade, downgrade or cancel any time from your account page, which opens Stripe's secure billing portal for the actual changes.

How does the annual discount work?

Toggle to Annual above and you're billed once a year at 20% less than the monthly price for that plan — the exact yearly total is shown under each price.

What payment methods are supported?

Checkout and payment method updates are handled entirely by Stripe's hosted pages — we never see or store your card details directly.

Is there a certification or compliance program?

Antenna is a real, early-stage, independently-built product and has not completed a formal SOC 2 or ISO 27001 audit. See our Security page for the honest details, or contact us if that's a hard requirement for your org.
