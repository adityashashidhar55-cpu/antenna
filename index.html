How it works

From raw signal to a foresight answer

No black box. Every score and status Antenna shows can be traced back to the source activity it was built from.

1

Ingest real signal

A live backend pulls activity from Hacker News, dev.to, Product Hunt and Reddit across India- and France-relevant topics — not synthetic or purchased panel data.

2

Score contextually

Each trend gets a 0–100 score derived from observed volume, velocity and cross-source corroboration — visible and re-computed as new signal arrives.

3

Classify momentum

Trends are tagged Exploding, Rising, Regular or Peaked based on their trajectory, so you can tell early movement apart from noise.

4

Ask the AI

A foresight chatbot answers questions grounded in the same stored trend and source data shown on the dashboard — with AI-derived notes always labeled as such.
