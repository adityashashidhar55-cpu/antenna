Keyword Research

Live Google Autocomplete suggestions for any keyword, plus AI-estimated search intent, difficulty, and content angles. This is a free-data alternative to paid tools like Semrush/Ahrefs — see the disclosure note on every result.

AI-SEO Website Analyzer

Checks a live website's real technical & content signals — schema.org structured data, AI-crawler access (robots.txt / llms.txt), heading structure, authority signals — then an AI classifies the site's category and gives prioritized recommendations for ranking better in AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). A free-data alternative to a paid Semrush-class GEO audit.

SEO Insights Library

A growing, browsable archive of AI-SEO analyses. A weekly automation runs the same analyzer above against a rotating set of real, freshly-launched sites and saves every report here — building toward a searchable library over time, at no subscription cost.
