Privacy Policy

A plain description of what data Antenna actually collects and why — no vague boilerplate, no claims of certifications that don't exist.

Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. Overview

Antenna is built to need as little of your personal data as possible. We don't run a traditional user-accounts system with passwords, and we don't use third-party advertising trackers. This page describes, honestly, exactly what is and isn't collected.

Honest framing: This policy describes our actual, real practices as an early-stage product — not a template padded out with clauses about certifications or audits we haven't undertaken. Where we're not sure or something doesn't apply, we say so plainly rather than guessing.

2. What We Collect

Concretely, here's what Antenna collects:

DataWhenWhy
Email addressOnly if you provide it — e.g. to activate a paid plan or sync your watchlist across devicesTo look up your plan/preferences and send billing or product-related communications

We do not require a username or password to use Antenna's core features. We do not collect your full payment card number — that never touches our systems (see Section 4). We do not run third-party advertising trackers, ad pixels, or sell your data to data brokers.

3. How We Use It

Where we do collect an email address or watchlist data, we use it only to:

  • Look up and apply your correct plan and feature access.
  • Sync your watchlist or workspace across devices or browser sessions, if you use that feature.
  • Send billing receipts and, occasionally, important product updates (you can opt out of non-essential communications).
  • Respond to support requests you send us directly.

4. Payment Data (Stripe)

All payment processing for Antenna is handled by Stripe through Stripe's own hosted Checkout flow. When you subscribe to a paid plan, you enter your card details directly into Stripe's interface — Antenna never sees, transmits, or stores your full card number, CVV, or other full payment card data. We may receive limited metadata from Stripe (such as your subscription status, plan tier, and a masked/last-4 card reference) so we can grant you the correct access.

Stripe's own handling of your payment data is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Antenna primarily uses your browser's local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to keep your watchlist, workspace projects, and UI preferences on your device between visits. This data stays in your browser unless you explicitly use a sync feature that sends it to our backend tied to your email. Clearing your browser's site data will remove it.

We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

6. Third Parties

Antenna relies on a small number of third-party services to actually run:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts the static site you're reading right now.
  • n8n (cloud) — our backend automation platform, which serves live trend, product, and startup data to the dashboard.
  • Stripe — processes all payments for paid plans, as described above.

We don't use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs beyond what's described here. If that changes, we'll update this policy.

7. Retention

We retain email and billing-related data for as long as needed to provide the Service and to meet reasonable accounting/legal obligations. Watchlist data stored only in your browser persists until you clear it yourself. If you'd like your data deleted, contact us — see Section 11.

8. Your Choices

You can:

  • Use Antenna's free features without ever providing an email address.
  • Clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove your locally-stored watchlist and preferences.
  • Ask us to delete any email-linked data we hold about you, or to export it, by emailing us.
  • Cancel a paid subscription at any time through the billing flow or by contacting us.

9. Children's Privacy

Antenna is a B2B research and strategy tool, not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

As Antenna evolves, this policy may change. We'll update the "Last updated" date above whenever we make a material change.
